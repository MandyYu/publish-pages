Easy Words — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026.

Operator and contact

Easy Words is operated by 于园园 (individual developer). For privacy questions, contact gf.yuanyuan@gmail.com.

Learning data on your device

The app does not require a developer-managed account. Your nickname, join date, learning progress, saved word cards, review records and preferences are stored on your device to provide the learning features. The current app does not send these learning records to a developer-operated server. It has no advertising, behavioural analytics or third-party tracking SDKs.

Purchases

Apple processes course payments. The app reads verified StoreKit transaction information to grant and restore access to purchased courses. The developer does not receive your payment-card details or Apple Account password through the app. Apple's services operate under Apple's own privacy terms.

Pronunciation

The app plays bundled British English audio files locally. It does not request microphone access or record your voice.

Support messages

If you email support, we receive the email address, message and attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond to your request and resolve issues. The mailbox is hosted by an email service provider; messages may be processed where that provider operates. Do not send passwords, full payment details or unnecessary personal information. We keep support correspondence only as needed to handle the request and meet applicable obligations. We do not use it for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Learning records remain on your device unless removed through device or app deletion, subject to any backup managed by your device. We do not provide our own learning-record cloud sync. Restore Purchases restores eligible course access, not local learning records. Contact us to request access, correction or deletion of information you sent to support, or exercise other rights available under applicable law. We cannot remotely retrieve learning records held only on your device. Apple handles purchase history under its own policies.

Students and families

Students and adults may use the app. There is no public profile, social sharing of learning records or in-app chat. A parent or guardian may contact us about information their child sent to support. Purchases remain subject to the device's purchase controls.

Changes

If the app's data practices change, we will update this policy and the relevant app disclosures.

Public website hosting

Our privacy and support pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. We do not add analytics, advertising trackers, contact forms or non-essential cookies to these pages. GitHub processes hosting information under its privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

Your choices and rights

You may contact us at gf.yuanyuan@gmail.com about personal information you send to support. Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability, object to processing, or withdraw consent where consent is the basis. In the European Economic Area you may also complain to your local data protection authority. We use support correspondence to respond to a request, pursue our legitimate interest in resolving app issues, and meet legal obligations where applicable. We do not sell personal information or use support messages for targeted advertising. Apple, GitHub and our email provider may process information in countries other than yours, under their respective terms and safeguards.
