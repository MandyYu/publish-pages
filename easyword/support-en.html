Restore a course

Open My, then the current course's unlock card, and choose Restore Purchases. Use the Apple Account that made the purchase. A purchase grants access to that course only; learning prerequisites still apply.

Payment completed but access is missing

Check your connection and try Restore Purchases. If the problem remains, email the app version, device/iOS version, course name and an error screenshot with personal details removed. Do not send your Apple password or payment-card information.

The next course is locked

Complete every lesson in the preceding course and collect the lesson cards. The Foundation Course has two free parts; later courses have one free part after they become available. Remaining parts are purchased separately for each course.

British English pronunciation

Check your volume and audio output. The app uses the system's British English voice. If it is unavailable, follow the app's message to prepare an English (United Kingdom) voice in your device's speech settings. The app does not record your voice.

Learning records and changing devices

Learning records are local. Restore Purchases does not restore your progress. Do not delete the app to troubleshoot missing records without considering this limitation. The public app currently has no learning-backup export or import entry.

Refund requests

For eligible purchase issues, visit https://reportaproblem.apple.com/ and follow Apple's instructions. Apple determines eligibility and the outcome; support cannot promise a refund.

Language

Use My → App Language to open the app's system settings, where available. Review uses phonetic prompts in English and translated words in Spanish or Chinese.
