Privacy policy

Effective October 31, 2026. This policy covers Moo Word Hunt for iOS.

Your game

The app stores puzzle progress, coins, reward receipts and sound and vibration preferences locally. It does not require an account or provide a developer-operated cloud save.

Ads and privacy choices

We use Google AdMob for optional rewarded ads and Google’s User Messaging Platform to check applicable consent requirements. This check can contact Google when the app opens. An ad is requested when you choose to watch one and the consent platform allows it.

Depending on your choices, region and SDK configuration, Google may process IP addresses and approximate location, device identifiers, ad impressions and interactions, performance and diagnostic information for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. See Google’s privacy policy. Where available, open Settings → Ad privacy options to review your choices. The puzzles can be played without watching ads.

Sharing and contacting support

Sharing opens the iOS share sheet. You choose the destination and whether to send. If you email support, we receive your address and the information you send to respond to your request.

Control and contact

Removing the app deletes its local game data from the device. For questions or requests about information you have sent to us, contact the developer at mandyyu_f@163.com.
