Player support

How do I find a word?

Swipe along a straight line, or tap the first and last letters. Words may run backwards or diagonally. Tap Next level when you finish.

Where is my progress?

Your device saves the current puzzle and coins automatically. No account is needed. Removing the app may remove the save.

Why didn’t I get an ad reward?

Optional ads need a connection and available inventory. Wait for the ad to confirm the reward before closing it. One earned reward adds 50 coins. You can keep playing without ads.
